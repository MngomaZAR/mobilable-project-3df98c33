# Papzii pilot: iOS 42 / Android 5

This is a restricted live-server pilot, not a public or financially accepted release.

## Install

- iPhone/iPad: open the existing TestFlight invitation, select Papzii and update to 1.0.0 (42). Apple confirmed external beta availability.
- Android: join the existing internal test at https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701137796431968292 using the invited Google account, then update to version code 5. Play accepted version 5 as a completed internal release. Availability can take time to propagate.
- If the update is missing, check the store account and exact build number. Do not judge the latest changes from build 41 or version 4.
- Sign in with your own existing account. Do not share passwords or verification documents over email.

## Two-person test

1. Sign in, sign out, sign in again and verify profile details survive reopening the app.
2. Existing client accounts can use Settings > Edit, select Photographer or Model, read/accept the Creator Policy and tap Apply as a creator. Complete the in-app identity process. Tester membership does not confer approval or admin access.
3. Add your own rights-cleared profile/portfolio, service area, prices and availability. An authorized admin must review real identity evidence before creator discovery and booking eligibility. Do not upload synthetic identity evidence to production.
4. After actual approval, use the other person's independent account to find the creator on Home/search/map, open the profile, select a service and future South African appointment time, and submit a scheduled request.
5. The creator opens Bookings and accepts. Both accounts must show the same booking and status after refreshing/reopening. Accepted does not mean paid or completed.
6. Open the booking chat from both devices, send messages, verify persistence, go offline briefly, reconnect and check for duplicates or missing updates.
7. Test map search, marker selection, locate with permission granted/denied, sheet dragging from its handle, scrolling the list, portrait/landscape, tablet and keyboard appearance. Verify controls and labels do not overlap. Browser screenshots do not prove native gestures.
8. Check Feed, Settings, profile edit, reviews, privacy/permissions, support, legal pages, notification taps and back navigation. Verify unavailable features say so rather than showing false success.
9. Report issues with build, device/OS, role, screen, reproduction steps, expected/actual result and screenshot or recording through TestFlight feedback and the in-app support route. Do not include passwords, ID numbers or bank details.

## Explicit limits

- Payments, refunds, creator bank payouts, video calls, instant dispatch and digital purchases are unavailable for general use. Do not attempt payments or enter bank details in this pilot.
- The 30% platform / 70% creator split is configured server-side; that is not proof of bank settlement. Processing costs come from the platform share.
- Native push/media delivery, device gestures and live financial settlement still need acceptance evidence. Public privacy/terms hosting and payout-provider arrangements remain open.
- Admin screens in the attached screenshots demonstrate synthetic QA flows only. They do not grant testers administrative permissions or prove anyone's real identity was approved.
