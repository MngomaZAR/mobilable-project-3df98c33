Papzii Community Guidelines

Last Updated: 13 October 2026

✅ Do

  • Treat all users with respect and professionalism
  • Communicate clearly about booking expectations
  • Pay on time and honour commitments
  • Report violations using the in-app report function

❌ Don't

  • Harass, threaten, or discriminate against any user
  • Share others' personal information without consent
  • Post fake reviews or manipulate ratings
  • Solicit off-platform payments to avoid fees
  • Post content depicting minors inappropriately

Consequences

Violations may result in warnings, content removal, suspension, or permanent bans.
