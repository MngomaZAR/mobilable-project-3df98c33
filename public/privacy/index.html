Papzii Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13 October 2026

1. Responsible Party

PAPZ (Pty) Ltd (registration 2025/188861/07) is the Responsible Party under POPIA 4 of 2013. Information Officer: privacy@papzii.co.za.

2. Personal Information We Collect

You provide: Name, email, phone, profile picture, location, portfolio images, payment details (via PayFast), government ID (for verified accounts), messages.

Automatically: Device IDs, OS, app version, usage analytics, push notification tokens.

3. Why We Process Your Information

  • Contractual necessity: Facilitating bookings, payments, disputes
  • Legitimate interest: Security, fraud prevention, service improvement
  • Consent: Marketing, location tracking (withdraw anytime in Settings)
  • Legal obligation: FICA, tax reporting, law enforcement requests

4. Location Data

With permission, location supports discovery and service availability. Private live booking locations are available only to booking participants and authorised administrators, after payment and acceptance, from two hours before the scheduled start until the scheduled end. Only fixes from the last five minutes are displayed as live. Stopping live display does not automatically erase stored records; deletion requests and applicable retention requirements are handled separately.

5. How We Share Your Information

We share only with: other users (per your settings), PayFast (payment processing), Expo/Firebase (push notifications), backend infrastructure and cloud storage processors, and legal authorities when required by South African law.

We never sell your personal information.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (subject to legal retention)
  • Object to direct marketing
  • Complain to the Information Regulator: inforeg@justice.gov.za | +27 10 023 5200

7. Security

We use TLS for transport, authenticated access checks, restricted identity-document links and encrypted bank details. No system can guarantee complete security. Security incidents and privacy requests can be raised through in-app support.

8. Contact

privacy@papzii.co.za
